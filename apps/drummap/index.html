Web port of DrumMap — a 2-channel Grids pattern generator for Hemisphere by Ben Rosenbach, Phazerville firmware, based on Mutable Instruments Grids by Émilie Gillet (pattern data GPL-3.0). ReadDrumMap(), chaos, thresholds, CV assignment and auto-reset are ported 1:1.
