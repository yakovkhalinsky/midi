Shared-clock rack for the web sequencer ports (TB-3PO, DrumMap, Melogen). The three OLEDs at the top are the live slots; pick one (SLOT selector or click a screen) to edit its controls underneath. One clock, one output destination — each slot keeps its own clock division, mute and per-slot MIDI channel/velocity. Rack settings are independent of the standalone app pages. If a slot is silent on first RUN, click anywhere on the page once (browser autoplay policy) — the RUN button counts as the gesture.
