Web port of TB-3PO — a random generator of TB-303 style acid patterns by Phazerville (applet © 2020 Logarhythm, mods by djphazer, MIT license; quantizer & scales by Émilie Gillet / Bryan Head). Pattern generation, PRNG and quantizer are ported from the Teensy 4.x firmware; pitch CV maps to 1 V/oct with C4 = 0 V.
